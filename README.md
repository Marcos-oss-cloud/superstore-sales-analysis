---

# 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes sales data from a retail company to identify patterns related to products, customers, discounts, regions, shipping modes, and profitability.

The main objective is to transform raw sales data into actionable business insights that support data-driven decision making.

---

# 🎯 Objectives

- Clean and prepare the dataset
- Explore business performance through data analysis
- Identify opportunities to improve profitability
- Generate business-oriented insights
- Develop a complete EDA portfolio project

---

# ❓ Business Questions

This project answers the following business questions:

1. Which product category generates the highest sales revenue?
2. Which product category generates the highest total profit?
3. Which sub-categories generate the highest sales and profit?
4. Does discount affect profitability?
5. Which customer segment generates the highest sales and profit?
6. Which regions and states generate the highest sales and profit?
7. How have sales and profits evolved over time?
8. Which shipping mode performs best?
9. Who are the most valuable customers?
10. Which products generate the highest sales and profit?

# 💡 Key Business Insights

- Technology generated the highest sales and profit among product categories.
- Copiers were the most profitable sub-category.
- Discounts above approximately 30% were associated with lower profitability.
- Consumer was the company's most valuable customer segment.
- The West region achieved the strongest business performance.
- Sales and profits generally increased between 2014 and 2017.
- High sales do not necessarily result in high profit.
- The Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier was the best-performing product.

---

# 📋 Business Recommendations

- Review discount strategies to avoid excessive reductions in profit.
- Prioritize investment in high-margin products.
- Investigate products with high sales but low profitability.
- Strengthen relationships with highly profitable customers.
- Prepare inventory and marketing campaigns for seasonal demand.
- Replicate successful regional strategies in lower-performing markets.
